What we check

Five things a follower count does not show you.

  • TikTok is pushing their videos

    Their videos reach far more people than most creators their size. You pay a small creator's fee and reach a bigger audience.

  • Their followers trust them

    They rarely post ads, so when they recommend you, people listen instead of scrolling past.

  • They catch trends early

    They join new trends before everyone else does, so people see your product while the trend is still growing.

  • Their followers already buy from them

    They already sell through a TikTok Shop, a shop link or a discount code. Their followers buy, not just watch.

  • They look and sound like the big names, for less

    Tell us a famous creator you like. We find smaller ones with the same style and the same kind of followers, for a fraction of the fee.
