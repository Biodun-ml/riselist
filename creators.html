For creators: your data, your choice

For TikTok creators: what Riselist holds about you, where it came from, how to claim your profile, and how to see, correct or remove your data.

Riselist helps brands find creators for paid partnerships. To do that, we keep a database of public TikTok creator profiles. You may be in it even though you never signed up with us. This page explains, in plain words, what we have and how you can see it, fix it, or remove it.

What we collect

Only things anyone can see on your public TikTok profile without logging in:

  • your username, display name, profile photo and bio
  • your follower, following and like counts
  • your latest 30 public videos: views, likes, comment count, shares, sounds, hashtags, captions and dates
  • whether a post looks sponsored (for example a "Paid partnership" label or #ad)
  • links in your bio (including shop links like TikTok Shop or an Amazon storefront)
  • a business email only if you put it in your bio

We also calculate a few scores from those numbers — like your average views compared to your followers, how often you post ads, and whether you sell products. These are estimates by our software, not judgements about you, and they can be wrong.

What we never collect: private accounts, direct messages, passwords, phone numbers, home addresses, or details about your followers. We don't guess things like your health, religion, ethnicity or sexuality, and we don't use face recognition. We don't knowingly include anyone under 18.

Where it came from

From your public TikTok profile and videos. We check for updates about once a week.

Why we have it

So brands can discover creators who fit their campaigns and send you a business enquiry at the email you made public. That can mean paid work for you. Brands that use us agree not to spam you, not to resell your data, and to stop contacting you if you ask.

Our legal reason is called "legitimate interests" — the data is public business information you chose to share, and we use it in a limited way linked to that purpose. You can always say no (see below).

Who sees it

Paying brand and agency customers of Riselist, and the service providers who help us run the website (like our hosting company). We never sell it to data brokers.

Your choices

You want to... What happens
See your data We send you everything we hold about you, including where it came from.
Fix something We correct it. (Most numbers update automatically each week.)
Remove your data We delete your profile, take it out of search, and put you on our do-not-collect list so you are not added back at the weekly refresh.
Stop brands emailing you We hide your email from brands. If you want a full removal, choose "Remove".

How to ask

  1. Email us at samuelaguda77@gmail.com with the subject "Creator data request" and your TikTok username. (A one-click form is coming; email works right now.)
  2. We confirm by email when it is done.

To make sure it's really you, we may ask you to either email us from the address in your bio, or put a short code we send you in your TikTok bio for a day. We only use this to check your request. It's free and you don't need to give a reason.

How fast we respond

  • Removal requests: done within 7 days.
  • All other requests: answered within 30 days.
  • We'll email you to confirm when it's done.

What the do-not-collect list keeps

Only the bare minimum to recognise your account and keep you out — your TikTok username and a scrambled account ID. Nothing else. If you change your username, let us know so we can update it.

Please note: if a brand had already contacted you before you asked to be removed, they may still have your email in their own inbox. We will tell our customers about your request, and our terms require them to stop contacting you through Riselist.

Claim your profile

Want brands to find you? Soon you will be able to connect your TikTok account to Riselist in one step, using TikTok's official login. That gives us only your public profile and video numbers, and it lets you see exactly what brands see.

We are still setting up TikTok's official login, so for now the fastest way is to email us at samuelaguda77@gmail.com with your TikTok username and the kind of brands you would like to work with. We will look at your public profile and add you by hand.

Not happy?

Talk to us first at samuelaguda77@gmail.com — we'll try to sort it out. You can also complain to your data protection authority, for example:

  • Nigeria: Nigeria Data Protection Commission — ndpc.gov.ng
  • UK: Information Commissioner's Office — ico.org.uk
  • EU: your national authority (list at edpb.europa.eu)
  • California: California Privacy Protection Agency — cppa.ca.gov
